# Version 0.4.0: observable-preserving finite-model reduction

This release adds a response-operator experiment that retains all three internal coordinates and original nonlinear contact-energy observables of three-node models. Three geometries, three stiffnesses and two grids produce 18 projection instances at three times; nine additional domain references complete the 27-instance campaign.

The pre-execution rank rule admits 17/18 instances by calculated error bounds. Sixteen also meet the total cold-cost gate; all 18 have measured response error below 0.002. All nine grid checks and all nine domain checks fail 0.001, so these are finite-matrix results, not converged physical or biological predictions. Both exceptions and every checkpoint remain in the record.

An independent full same-host replay retains the same acceptance decisions and agrees over 27 archives and 1,065 arrays to maximum absolute difference 1.17e-15. Six existing development references agree with immutable v0.3.0 content within 1.69e-14. Cold timing ratios are machine- and workload-specific; they are not cross-platform software-pass criteria.

The original-grid direction-register design passes stored nine-state walk/control algebra checks, but its first native overlap exceeds the declared million-operation cap. Complete readout and matched two-address comparison were not reached. They do not provide an encoding of the projected operator, a completed primary protein circuit, biological validation or quantum advantage. See the experiment's explicit completion status and procedural amendment.

The original v0.3.0 scientific source/data/results remain unchanged. New verification routines compare physical response arrays; gauge-dependent projected matrices retain their full same-host replay record separately. Existing Gaussian-response and independent-family validation campaigns remain proposed.
