# Project Pulsar: executable four-target validation protocol

12 September 2026. This is a versioned development protocol, not a claim of prospective registration or completed four-target validation. The selected structures and pocket identities were already known during protocol development. Freezing decisions now prevents further outcome-driven changes; it does not make the present comparisons blinded. Protein scoring is being performed separately in `../pilot/`. This companion specifies the remaining validation work and records input preparation and the executed Ohm comparator.

## The prediction and its evaluation answer different questions

The response matrix quantifies a mechanical model's sensitivity to local contact stiffening. It does not directly measure binding affinity, functional inhibition or a clinical effect. For each candidate residue, the ranking uses the root-mean-square normalized response over a fixed functional receiver. The primary biological question is whether this ranking concentrates experimentally observed allosteric-ligand contacts near its top. The physical-model question is whether the nonlinear law improves this prioritization over matched harmonic and distance-Hookean laws. The solver question is whether quantum estimation reproduces the same discrete calculation at a useful, fully recorded cost. Each question has its own result and failure condition.

## Input records are fixed before score calculation

`build_validation_inputs.py` derives the domains, complete residue maps, functional receivers, solvent exposure and candidate masks from cached input structures and UniProt/PDBe SIFTS records. It does not read either evaluation structure or response scores. `prepared/input-freeze.json` binds the preparation code and each generated input record. All changes after this freeze require a dated amendment stating the reason and whether any scores or labels had already been inspected.

The script keeps one deposited coordinate model and the selected conformer of each disordered atom, using Biopython's maximum-occupancy selection. It retains author chain, author residue number, insertion code, label chain, label sequence position, reference-sequence accession and reference position. A coordinate row is never equated with a sequence position. Residues lacking a resolved alpha-carbon are excluded and recorded; sequence gaps receive no artificial backbone contact. Cloning residues outside the sequence mapping are excluded. Ligands, metals, solvent and DNA provide annotations where specified below but do not become mechanical nodes. Removing them does not produce an experimentally apo conformation.

### KRAS uses the selected GDP-conditioned KRAS4B structure

The primary model is 4OBE author chain A, residues 1–166, with 166 resolved alpha-carbons. Its sequence matches **P01116-2 (KRAS4B)**. The displayed canonical P01116 sequence is KRAS4A; therefore a bare claim of complete identity to that displayed sequence would be wrong. The chosen 1–166 range is a protocol definition of the modeled catalytic fragment; the deposited chain extends further. Chain B and the terminal extension are sensitivity inputs, not extra independent proteins.

The functional receiver comprises every retained protein residue with a heavy atom within 4.0 Å of the **GDP in input chain A**: **11–18, 28, 30, 32, 116, 117, 119, 120, 145–147**. This is a nucleotide-site receiver defined without MOV contacts. GDP and Mg are removed after annotation. The evaluation label is the heavy-atom contact set within 5.0 Å of **MOV in 6OIM author chain A**. GDP, Mg and water in that reference are not allosteric positive labels. 6OIM contains the G12C inhibitor-bound state, whereas 4OBE has no G12C mutation. This is a retrospective cross-state, cross-variant contact-recovery test, not proof that the wild-type structure possesses the drug-bound pocket with the same population or affinity. [RCSB 4OBE](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/4OBE); [RCSB 6OIM](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/6OIM); [UniProt P01116](https://rest.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P01116.json).

This excludes the regulatory SH3/SH2 assembly from the primary model. A separate static sensitivity analysis retains the complete resolved author chain A; results from that larger construct are not independent replicate proteins. The input contains myristate and P16 and is ligand-conditioned. It also contains **D363N in current P00519 numbering (author D382N)**, explicitly described as engineered in the CIF. The model therefore does not represent wild-type catalytic chemistry.

The receiver comprises retained heavy-atom contacts within 4.0 Å of **input P16**: current P00519 positions **248, 249, 253, 256, 269–271, 286, 290, 313, 315–318, 321, 370, 380–382**. This uses the input inhibitor-occupied functional pocket; UniProt ATP-binding and catalytic annotations provide a separately specified receiver sensitivity, not an outcome-tuned replacement. The allosteric label is the mapped contact set within 5.0 Å of **AY7 (asciminib) in 5MO4 author chain A**. **NIL (nilotinib) is excluded from that label.** The reference has T315I and D363N in current numbering, corresponding to the deposited T334I/D382N names. Sequence maps, mutations, absent residues and domain-excluded contacts are reported individually. [RCSB 1OPL](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1OPL); [RCSB 5MO4](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/5MO4); [UniProt P00519](https://rest.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P00519.json).

### Cardiac myosin retains prediction while its selected label is unresolved

The primary MYH7 model is 5TBY author chain A, **P12883 positions 85–778**, the annotated myosin motor domain. This 694-residue model omits the N-terminal SH3-like region, lever arm, light chains and interacting partner head; the full resolved heavy-chain head and its deposited protein partners are required static sensitivity cases before interpreting long-range effects as assembly-level behavior. The receiver is the UniProt ATP-binding segment **178–185**. This is a curated functional annotation, not an ATP-contact measurement from 5TBY, which contains no ATP ligand.

5TBY is a homology-derived human beta-cardiac interacting-heads model fitted to a **20 Å** reconstruction. It is not an atomic-resolution experimental measurement of every side chain. Consequently, contact masks, exposure and any atomistic comparator are provisional for this input. Primary prediction still supplies a complete modeled-domain response matrix and five ranked MYH7 residues with confidence and scope annotations. [RCSB 5TBY](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/5TBY); [UniProt P12883](https://rest.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P12883.json).

The selected 6C1H reference contains **rat myosin-Ib**, with rabbit actin and calmodulin; it is not a cardiac MYH7–mavacamten reference. No ligand-contact benchmark is constructed by transferring its contacts as though they belonged to MYH7. The MYH7 slot remains unresolved in the primary test family. A corrected reference can enter only after a documented reference correction or an explicitly justified, separately recorded alternate MYH7 reference, before scoring that amended endpoint. If clarification does not arrive, deliver the MYH7 matrix, top five and structural visualization and state that this endpoint was not evaluable. [RCSB 6C1H](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/6C1H).

### MYC retains MAX and restricts claims to the resolved DNA-recognition fragment

The primary input is the **1NKP author A/B MYC–MAX pair**, retaining MYC **P01106 positions 368–449** and MAX **P61244 positions 23–102**. The primary matrix has 162 nodes: 82 MYC residues and 80 MAX residues. Only MYC residues enter the requested MYC top five. Current SIFTS positions differ from the legacy MYC numbering in the deposited DBREF: current positions 368–449 correspond to author **900–981** and legacy positions 353–434. The output preserves all identifiers instead of silently choosing one convention.

The receiver is the set of mapped MYC residues with heavy atoms within 4.0 Å of the associated **DNA author chains F/G**, measured before DNA removal: current positions **370, 371, 374–379, 381–383, 386, 406–409**. MAX stays in the protein contact network because the input is a partner-bound heterodimer. The alternate author D/E pair is a static structural sensitivity, not a second independent biological target. [RCSB 1NKP](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1NKP); [PDBe SIFTS 1NKP](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pdbe/api/mappings/uniprot/1nkp).

This fragment cannot validate full-length unbound MYC or disordered regions absent from the model. A single partner-bound conformation does not establish an equilibrium ensemble. MYC has no designated allosteric ligand-contact truth set in this protocol. Consensus across static methods, retained conformations and domain choices will measure robustness. If docking is attempted, it will test geometric accessibility on declared static structures with fixed ligand preparation and search settings; a docking score or method consensus is not evidence of allosteric modulation. MYC remains descriptive in the biological validation results.

## Candidate masks separate distal prediction from surface-pocket interpretation

For the primary top-five ranking, a residue must belong to the target chain and retained sequence interval, lie outside the functional receiver, and have a minimum alpha-carbon distance **at least 6 Å** from every receiver residue. The full matrix still includes receiver and otherwise ineligible residues. The same universe is used by every energy model and comparator. The 4 Å and 8 Å distal masks are predeclared sensitivities. These are geometric operational definitions, not literature-established universal boundaries of allostery. Excluding the directly perturbed receiver follows the need to avoid measuring an intervention at the intervention site; comparable receiver exclusions appear in AlloPred and Ohm (Greener and Sternberg, 2015; Wang et al., 2020).

Surface exposure does **not** silently filter the primary all-residue ranking. Solvent-accessible area is calculated on each retained protein subsystem using Shrake–Rupley, a 1.4 Å probe and 960 sphere points per atom. Relative area divides by the amino-acid-specific theoretical maxima of Tien et al. (2013). The declared surface mask is RSA≥0.20, with 0.10/0.30 sensitivities. This numerical threshold is a protocol choice. It is used for a separately labelled surface-only shortlist and for matching geometric control characteristics. Domain truncation can expose an artificial surface; full-subsystem sensitivity and distances from truncation boundaries must be shown. A surface residue is not thereby a pocket or a verified drug-binding site. [Tien et al., 2013](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0080635); [Biopython SASA implementation](https://biopython.org/docs/latest/api/Bio.PDB.SASA.html).

For an eligible evaluation reference, each ligand-contact residue is mapped by sequence and intersected with the primary candidate universe. The evaluation file reports four sets: all ligand contacts, mapped contacts, retained-domain contacts and eligible contacts. It lists every removal and its reason. Missing or receiver-overlapping positive residues do not disappear from the denominator without disclosure. If no eligible contacts remain, the endpoint is unresolved; neither the receiver nor the mask is changed to rescue the result.

## The primary test family has fixed slots and a finite null distribution

The primary inferential family has **three one-sided tests**, one each for KRAS, ABL and MYH7. Each tests whether the mean full-model score of the eligible ligand-contact set exceeds an input-geometry-matched random-set null. The MYH7 test stays unresolved with p=1 until its reference is eligible; it is not deleted after seeing results. MYC has no ligand-contact endpoint and is not inserted into this family. Holm correction controls the declared family's error rate at 0.05 under valid null p-values (Holm, 1979). A rejection is evidence of association conditional on the matching assumptions, not a claim of biological causation or generalization to unseen proteins.

For target t, let P be its eligible contact set, m its size, U its candidate universe and S_i its fixed score. The statistic is **T(P)=mean(S_i for i in P)**. Before response evaluation, partition U by degree tertile, receiver-distance tertile and RSA<0.20/≥0.20. Tertile boundaries are the one-third and two-thirds quantiles of the input-only features; duplicate boundaries collapse. The null retains the observed number m_b of contact labels in every stratum b. It samples subsets of size m_b uniformly from that stratum's n_b candidates. The observed label set is itself an attainable configuration. The null has **M=product_b choose(n_b,m_b)** distinct configurations. It controls those coarse measured features; it does not remove every structural, spatial or evolutionary confounder.

If M≤100,000, enumerate all configurations, including the observed set, and use **p=count(T_null≥T_observed)/M**. Otherwise, draw 10,000 distinct configurations from the remaining attainable space with NumPy PCG64 seed **20260912**, preserving each stratum count, and use **p=(1+count(T_null≥T_observed))/10001**. Ties count against rejection. This distinction between exhaustive and sampled permutations follows the discrete-null treatment of Phipson and Smyth (2010). Report M, the actual number of unique draws, the seed, quantile cut points, stratum counts, null quantiles and the observed effect. A degenerate null with M=1 gives p=1. The smallest exact p is 1/M; under the three-test family the first Holm threshold is 0.05/3, so M must reach 60 even to make a first rejection attainable. Repeated draws cannot create new independent pockets or proteins.

The null randomizes positions of the complete positive label set under conditional exchangeability. It is a conditional random-label model, not an experimental randomization. Residues share one protein and correlated scores; they are not independent biological replicates. Alongside this test, report precision at five, any-contact hit, all five ligand distances, the full ranked positive-contact positions and an enrichment effect against the matched null. Do not replace these interpretable values with a p-value alone.

### Negative pockets form a separate evidence gate

No experimentally evidenced non-functional pocket set for the exact KRAS, ABL, MYH7 or MYC contexts has been verified by this research. The current register is therefore **empty and unresolved for all four targets**. This is a limitation, not a statement that such pockets do not exist. Unannotated fpocket/Ohm pockets, inactive compounds, non-binding molecules and low model scores are not verified non-functional pockets. In particular, a bound ligand must have been shown not to modulate the specified functional readout at adequate exposure; missing binding or inadequate occupancy cannot establish a pocket-level negative.

Each proposed negative entry must record the structure, sequence/variant, ligand, exact pocket-contact residues, direct binding or occupancy evidence, functional assay and readout, tested concentration/occupancy range, measured effect with uncertainty, matched positive assay control, publication and compatibility with the current domain/receiver. The entry is eligible only if the assay could have detected the proposed functional effect and the cited study supports the negative under those conditions. Greener and Sternberg (2015) explicitly warn that unannotated training-negative pockets may retain undiscovered allosteric character. [AlloPred primary article](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12859-015-0771-1).

If eligible negatives are supplied, compare each pocket's mean score with the positive pocket on the same structure and report the number of distinct pockets. A pocket-level randomization test can enumerate the finite exchangeable pocket assignments only when matching and sample size support it. With one positive and K exchangeable negative pockets its smallest one-sided p is 1/(K+1); 10,000 resamples do not change that floor. No negative-pocket significance claim is made from a handful of pockets. The absence of these controls prevents a broad claim of allosteric specificity even if the primary matched-residue test succeeds. Additional evidenced controls can enter only through a dated amendment that records when their labels and scores were inspected.

## Model and predictor comparisons retain their own scope

Every target uses identical coordinates, contacts, retained basis, grid and functional receiver across the full, harmonic and distance-Hookean laws. Their observable and normalization remain the fixed biquadratic contact-energy observable and common harmonic standard deviations. Compare the finite-time response with the **equilibrium covariance limit** to test whether temporal relaxation adds useful ranking information. Compare degree-normalized local intervention with a declared equal-per-contact fractional-stiffening sensitivity so that a benefit is not explained solely by node degree.

For a formal nonlinear-prioritization claim, use a separately declared family of **six** directional tests: each evaluable target's full-versus-harmonic and full-versus-Hookean improvement, with MYH7 slots reserved/unresolved. Convert each method's scores to tie-aware percentile ranks within the common universe. The statistic is the mean paired percentile-rank improvement on P, tested against the same geometry-matched label configurations. Percentile ranks prevent a uniform amplitude increase from masquerading as improved prioritization. Holm correction is 0.05 within this secondary family. Report paired precision-at-five and ranking changes regardless of significance. This secondary family does not convert a failed primary endpoint into success.

The established static-structure comparator is **Ohm**, which derives allosteric communication from contacts and active-site information (Wang et al., 2020). The current public upstream implementation has been built and executed on the exact KRAS domain and functional receiver; `ohm/execution.json` records it. The pinned commit is **462a3b1318e24ebe2061137fe88af719ef89c0ae**. The core license is GPL-3.0. Its compatibility patch updates two range-adaptor base declarations for GCC 15/C++23 and does not change numerical formulas. The current code differs from the 2020 paper in contact exclusions, symmetry, alpha handling and score normalization; it is reported as the **pinned upstream implementation**, not an exact reproduction of the paper. [Wang et al., 2020](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-020-17618-2); [official Ohm repository](https://bitbucket.org/dokhlab/ohm).

The KRAS Ohm run follows the current README: 3.4 Å atom contacts, CLI alpha 4.5 (effective alpha 0.5 in the inspected code), probability cutoff 0.05, 10,000 rounds and seed 11. The repeated seed 11 run is bitwise identical. Seeds 29/47 and a declared effective-alpha 3 sensitivity are retained. The current output is a path-normalized implementation score rather than the original paper's raw propagation frequency. Compare rankings and contact recovery on the shared universe; do not compare its magnitude with the thermal-response score. Ohm on ABL, MYH7 and MYC remains PoC work, with the low-resolution myosin caveat. The method uses all-atom information from the same static input, so report that informational difference from the Cα model.

AlloPred supplies a particularly close scientific precedent: it perturbs spring strengths at predicted pockets and measures normal-mode effects at the active site. Its learned pocket model is a literature comparator; no AlloPred result has been executed here. Simpler controls include contact degree, solvent exposure, distance from receiver and a fixed contact-network diffusion/PRS baseline. These diagnose geometry-driven rankings; they do not replace an established allostery predictor. All baseline parameters and failed executions remain in the output. No baseline imports molecular-dynamics trajectories.

## Four-target outputs are mandatory even when evaluation fails

Each target must produce a numerical N×N response matrix, a normalization record and a complete residue-map table. Primary modeled sizes are KRAS 166, ABL 252, MYH7 694 and MYC/MAX 162. The MYC matrix includes MAX context; the MYC shortlist contains only MYC residues. The deliverable must identify modeled sequence coverage, omitted residues and required subsystem scope. A modeled-domain matrix is not a full-length-protein matrix. If a downstream analysis requires different full-target indexing, provide an explicit mapping and missing-value entries instead of inventing response values for absent coordinates.

The five unique residue records include chain, author and reference position, score, eligibility mask, numerical uncertainty, ligand distance where evaluable, and whether the fifth–sixth boundary is resolved. A deterministic sort uses descending score, then reference position and chain; tied scores are marked rather than described as strict distinctions. Export the complete ranking as well. The 3D view shows the same coordinates, receiver, candidate mask, missing regions, five residues, evaluated pocket and legend, with an unambiguous color scale. A static annotated figure accompanies an inspectable structure/session artifact. The visual must not draw inferred contact labels onto unsupported residues.

The manifest contains input/reference/sequence/source hashes, code and environment versions, commands, seed list, hardware, elapsed time, peak memory, model and solver parameters, truncation/grid/domain checks, all comparator results, raw score vectors, numerical uncertainty and failed cases. Outputs contain no fabricated values where evaluation is unresolved. A clean environment reruns one target from cached public inputs and compares hashes and arrays within recorded tolerances; a second run confirms repeatability. Cross-machine differences and tolerance choices are recorded.

## Practical success requires separate numerical and biological evidence

A useful contact-recovery demonstration requires at least one eligible known-pocket residue among five for each evaluable target; two or more is the stretch goal. These are protocol-defined operational goals rather than empirically established biological thresholds. A convincing specificity claim additionally requires the declared matched-residue evidence, meaningful comparison with published and physical controls, and eligible negative-pocket evidence. Performance on one retrospective target does not establish transfer.

The current KRAS pilot must be interpreted against its full-coordinate harmonic reference. A high contact-recovery score cannot rescue a coordinate representation that fails the predeclared response-accuracy budget. If compression fails, the immediate PoC gate is to identify a resolved, affordable representation or establish the barrier. The quantum calculation is useful only if its measured/reconstructed response and ranking uncertainty meet the same accuracy requirement and the full preparation, operator, measurement and reconstruction costs are reported. A finite small circuit's success is not protein-level speedup or biological validation.
