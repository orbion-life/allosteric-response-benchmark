# Incremental biological information and Gaussian response computation

These studies add three complementary tests to the response benchmark: whether Gaussian scores provide information beyond structural controls, whether earlier snapshots preserve transient responses, and what a small native quantum response circuit costs.

- [Biological reanalysis](biology/README.md) reports all exploratory GRB2 and KRAS outcomes, held-out predictions and uncertainty.
- [Transient preservation](temporal/README.md) supplies four full numerical evidence packages and retains the KRAS orthogonality failure.
- [Six-qubit circuit study](quantum/README.md) includes compiled circuits, ideal checks, assumed-noise sensitivity and complete shot budgets.
- [Proposed amplification comparison](quantum/PROPOSED-AMPLIFICATION.md) defines the next local design study. It has not been run.

Large arrays and historical replays are release assets. See the [asset manifest](../../docs/evidence-v0.9.0/ASSET-MANIFEST-v0.9.0.json) for exact names and hashes. A software verification pass is distinct from a scientific acceptance outcome.

The [proposed nonlinear adequacy study](../../docs/evidence-v0.9.0/nonlinear-model-adequacy.md) compares unchanged Gaussian and harmonic controls with a qualified finite nonlinear triangle reference at predefined additional times. It introduces no new measured result.
