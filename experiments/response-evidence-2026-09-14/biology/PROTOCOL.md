# Incremental biological information in previously evaluated KRAS and GRB2 scores

Recorded 2026-09-14 before calculating the new partial-association or out-of-fold metrics. This is an exploratory reanalysis of already opened datasets. It is not preregistered biological validation, and its analysis choices were informed by the previously reported marginal correlations. All planned outcomes will be retained, regardless of direction.

1. Reuse the exact saved primary eligible masks, time-tau predictions and source-derived site aggregates. KRAS uses 110 distal residues and inverse-variance-weighted mean absolute RAF1-binding energy change; GRB2 C-terminal SH3 uses 21 distal residues and equal-weight mean absolute GAB2-binding energy change, each with at least ten admitted substitutions per site. Do not alter aggregation, receivers, structural inputs, eligibility, contacts, score time or labels. Verify stored masks and prior correlations before interpreting new calculations. These are associations with mutation sensitivity, not calibrated affinity predictions or supported pocket labels. Keep assays separate; do not pool differently aggregated outcomes.
2. Primary descriptive question: does Gaussian response carry an association with the continuous binding endpoint after linear adjustment of ranks for contact degree and nearest receiver C-alpha distance? Compute partial Spearman by residualizing Gaussian and outcome average ranks against an intercept plus degree and negative-distance ranks. Also report harmonic and Ohm on the identical mask, Gaussian after additionally controlling harmonic, and the existing folding-adjusted version as secondary sensitivity. Report the Gaussian-minus-harmonic partial-association contrast directly. Do not choose a favourable covariate set after the results.
3. Generalization diagnostic: partition the full model's sorted canonical residue list into five consecutive, approximately equal-length sequence sections using numpy.array_split, independently of outcomes. Hold out each section in turn. Apply all learned predictor and outcome transformations using only training sites: empirical mid-distribution percentiles (number of training observations strictly below plus half those equal, divided by training n), with test values transformed against the training distribution. Standardize predictors with training mean and population standard deviation. Fit linear ridge regression with unpenalized intercept and fixed alpha=1; do not tune hyperparameters. Compare (a) contact degree plus negative receiver distance, (b) that baseline plus Gaussian, (c) baseline plus harmonic, (d) baseline plus harmonic and Gaussian. No measured folding endpoint is included as a predictor because it would require another assay. Predictions are supervised exploratory combinations and not the original unsupervised Pulsar ranking.
4. Primary prediction contrast is relative reduction in out-of-fold mean squared error of binding percentile for adding Gaussian to degree-plus-distance; positive values favour addition. Also report pooled out-of-fold Spearman against the original continuous endpoint, each fold's paired error difference, and adding Gaussian after harmonic. Every site has exactly one prediction from a model trained without that entire section. Save training/held-out indices and fitted coefficients. This is within-protein sequence-block generalization, not independent-protein validation. Separated sequence positions can still be neighbours in three-dimensional space.
5. Uncertainty: draw 20000 paired ordinary site bootstrap samples and 20000 paired fixed-width sequence-block bootstrap samples (ten canonical positions per block anchored at the first model residue; sample nonempty blocks with replacement, preserving every selected block's admitted residues), with seed 2026091403 for KRAS and 2026091404 for GRB2. Recompute ranks and residualization within each bootstrap sample. Report percentile 95% and 97.5% intervals for the two assay-specific Gaussian partial associations; the latter offer a nominal Bonferroni familywise 95% across the two primary associations under the resampling assumptions. Also report paired intervals for the Gaussian-minus-harmonic contrast. Count degenerate samples, excluding only mathematically undefined results. Sequence-block intervals are coarse dependence sensitivity, not calibrated structural-cluster confidence. For prediction, bootstrap the fixed out-of-fold site loss pairs using the same blocks and report conditional paired loss-difference and relative-reduction intervals; these do not incorporate variability of model refitting across new datasets. Do not derive confirmatory p values or claim independent residues/proteins.
6. Descriptive shortlist comparison: retain each existing method's five largest scores, breaking ties by canonical position. Report residue indices, mean continuous binding magnitude and overlap. Compare Gaussian with degree and harmonic on the same mask. Do not invent a binary biological label, compute precision@5 without complete labels, or define positives by the opened outcome. Any shortlist difference remains unblinded and conditional on these sites. No threshold, model, time or score optimization follows this analysis.
7. Store this protocol and its SHA-256 before execution, exact input copies and hashes, source provenance references, script and environment versions, per-site fold assignment/predictions/losses, bootstrap draws, JSON summaries and a written interpretation. Limit BLAS threads to one. Run a fresh replay and independent algebraic spot checks.
