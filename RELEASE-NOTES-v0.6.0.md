# v0.6.0: Gaussian protein responses and nonlinear reference tests

This evidence update adds an executable four-target Gaussian workflow, a small quantum response estimator, and independent tests of the original nonlinear contact model. It preserves unsuccessful approximations and earlier replay failures. Agreement within a surrogate is distinguished from fidelity to the original model and from biological prediction.

## New evidence

- **Protein-scale Gaussian calculations.** Full-coordinate models of KRAS, ABL, MYC/MAX and cardiac myosin return complete signed response matrices, five-residue rankings and structural coordinates. The largest model has 694 residues and 2,076 internal coordinates; its complete calculation after prepared input took 249.19 seconds and 988.3 MB peak CPU memory. These costs apply to a fixed-centroid Gaussian equilibrium and its linear relaxation, using the original quartic contact-energy observables.
- **A Gaussian quantum-estimator prototype.** A rank-nine triangle projection supports a complete six-qubit estimator. For one residue pair at three specified times, 3,410,301 ideal simulator shots give maximum normalized response error below 0.000341 against the unreduced Gaussian reference. The maximum-order circuit uses 47,368 CX gates all-to-all or 119,616 with line routing. No hardware run or protein-scale quantum output is established.
- **An original nonlinear response reference.** Five finite grids, up to 29,531,250 states, pass both domain and both spacing comparisons at the unchanged 0.001 threshold. Independent propagators agree within 3.39×10⁻¹⁰. Direct positive and negative field switches at three shrinking amplitudes reproduce the response identity with maximum error 4.53×10⁻⁷ at the smallest amplitude. These checks concern a three-coordinate reflecting-box model at three registered times.
- **Original-density equilibrium and higher moments.** A separately registered continuation completes 32 sequential Monte Carlo (SMC) populations and passes the finite-box mean/covariance and per-kernel partition screens. Maximum error plus nominal simultaneous uncertainty is below 0.000512 for normalized means/covariances and 0.004725 relative for the partition integral. The separate cross-kernel partition diagnostic is 0.005327, so agreement at 0.005 remains unestablished. Independent quadrature also admits 15 selected differential/gradient moments; the two fixed samplers pass the separately declared 0.01 scale-normalized precision diagnostic. These moments do not certify a strong-generator residual or delayed response.
- **A retrospective functional comparison.** Across 110 distal KRAS sites, Gaussian scores correlate 0.299 with inferred RAF1-binding effect magnitude, versus 0.219 for harmonic response and −0.007 for Ohm. Contact degree correlates 0.348, and the adjusted Gaussian association is 0.084. Added biological value beyond structural controls remains unestablished.

## Preserved failures and scope

The original-measure local Hermite representation fails its 0.002 response gate: best rank-nine error is 0.05515 on the matched finite model. A separately declared diagnostic retaining all 87 tested functions still has error 0.04185. The fixed-centroid Gaussian itself differs from the original triangle response by 0.40930, with static-covariance error 0.40108.

Gaussian projections on KRAS and ABL pass the original three specified queries but fail additional early-time tests; the earlier-snapshot recovery also fails fresh confirmation. Whole-space nonlinear dynamics, original-density protein integration, independent-family biological improvement, noisy protein rankings and quantum advantage remain unestablished. Historical cross-platform bound failures remain. The asset set also includes the four previously unpublished stability archives; public availability requires completed upload and checksum verification.

Protocols, source, raw results, numerical costs and verification receipts remain separated by experiment. Use the accompanying reviewer guide and final archive manifest to identify the included files and optional large-particle data. Full simulator replay, saved-output verification and scientific acceptance are distinct operations.
