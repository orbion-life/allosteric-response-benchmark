# Tests of Gaussian centroid relaxation, compact quantum loading and independent protein prediction

Status: proposed experiments, 15 September 2026. No new experimental result is claimed in this protocol. Paths are relative to the repository root.

## A change to the Gaussian centroid tests one physical assumption

**Question.** Does fixing the Gaussian mean at the native structure account for an appreciable part of the original triangle's amplitude and ordering discrepancy? The first revision will optimize the mean as well as the covariance of the Gaussian. It will not enlarge the previously failed degree-six original-measure Hermite basis or represent that failed branch as new work. A shifted Gaussian remains a Gaussian approximation. Success on this fixture would not establish protein fidelity or biological usefulness.

**Fixed inputs.** Reuse the existing three-site triangle and its three positive harmonic coordinates, original quartic contact energy, native contact set, coordinate basis, inverse temperature, mobility and stiffness. Keep the original harmonic standard deviations and original harmonic time scale. Do not redefine contacts around the fitted mean or rescale each model by its own relaxation time. Preserve the existing finite-domain nonlinear reference and its recorded domain, spacing and independent-propagator discrepancies.

**Specified first model.** Write the internal coordinates as `q ~ N(m, Σ)` and minimize

`F(m,Σ) = E[U(q)] − log det(Σ)/(2β)`

with positive-definite covariance and the same quartic `U`. Use a Cholesky parameterization for covariance and constrain each harmonic-whitened mean coordinate to `|sqrt(βλ_k)m_k| ≤ 1`. This predetermined neighbourhood tests a modest centroid relaxation around the native reference rather than allowing migration to a remote minimum. It is a modelling constraint, not a biological tolerance. For contact displacement `a + Tq`, set `b = Tm`, `v = a+b`, `S = TΣTᵀ` and `z = |v|²−ℓ²`. Its exact Gaussian mean energy is

`κ/(8ℓ²) × [z² + 2z tr(S) + 4vᵀSv + 2tr(S²) + tr(S)²]`.

This expression follows by expanding the original contact polynomial and taking zero-mean Gaussian moments. It is the proposed implementation formula, not a fitted result. The original covariance-only objective is recovered at `m=0`. Keep all original model parameters fixed. Start from the existing covariance and three predetermined means: zero and positive/negative displacements of 0.1 original harmonic standard deviations along the softest retained coordinate. Choose among converged fits by variational objective only, never response error. Cap each start at 200 optimizer iterations. Save every objective, constrained stationarity residual, covariance spectrum, active mean bound and failure. Check derivatives by an independent finite-difference implementation. A fit that fails the constrained stationarity check or leaves covariance non-positive does not count as a model result. A solution on a mean bound is explicitly reported as constrained.

**Support boundary.** This Gaussian and its process have support on all internal-coordinate space, while the retained nonlinear reference uses a finite reflecting domain. Their equilibrium measures are therefore different. Retain this difference in the interpretation and report Gaussian mass outside each reference box, bounded from its one-coordinate Gaussian marginals. A small tail mass alone does not bound the quartic observable tail or establish dynamic agreement. Compare separately with both largest-box references at spacings 0.1 and 0.08, and retain the earlier domain-expansion discrepancies in the empirical error allowance. No support/domain convergence claim or fit to response labels is permitted. If observable-tail effects cannot be bounded, describe a discrepancy from the named finite reference rather than whole-space physical recovery.

**Dynamics and checks.** Define the proposed stationary Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process around `m` with drift `−μ(βΣ)⁻¹(q−m)` and diffusion `μ/β`. Evaluate the unchanged quartic residue observables with their shifted Gaussian means and lag covariances. Before comparing with nonlinear dynamics, check all static and delayed moment expressions by separately written Gaussian quadrature. Orders 5 and 7 in each independent standard-normal coordinate are the proposed exact-polynomial consistency checks, with a numerical discrepancy target of `10⁻⁸` in the original response normalization. The covariance and coordinate maps used by the two implementations must be recorded separately. This check concerns the new Gaussian calculation only.

Evaluate every entry of the three-by-three response at the retained ten times `t/τ = 0.0075, 0.025, 0.075, 0.1, 0.25, 0.75, 1, 2.5, 7.5, 10`. Report all 90 response entries, static covariances and delayed covariances beside the unchanged fixed-centroid Gaussian and harmonic controls. Use the finest stored nonlinear result, with its existing empirical reference allowance and independent propagation check. Do not run a new reference campaign merely to obtain a more favourable allowance.

**Decision and stop.** The amplitude acceptance rule remains observed discrepancy plus the existing reference allowance plus newly measured numerical allowance no greater than `0.002` for every entry at every time. For each receiver, report the other two sites' signed responses, absolute-response ordering and gap. An ordering is resolved only when its gap exceeds the stated uncertainty envelope. No top-five metric applies to a three-site fixture. Failure of either the amplitude requirement or any resolved ordering ends this particular remedy within the sprint. An unresolved reference comparison remains inconclusive. Report the fitted means and residual error even if fitting improves the variational objective. Do not promise that mean relaxation will meet `0.002`, and do not claim that it recovers non-Gaussian cumulants, multiple basins or whole-space dynamics.

**Allocation and delivery.** Proposed 1–21 December activity: at most 32 person-hours, suggested allocation Promit 24 and Aniruddh 8, and 24 CPU-worker hours. This is an allocation within the confirmed 532 person-hours and the existing 200 CPU-worker-hour ceiling, not additional committed effort. No GPU is required by this first test. End at the first violated correctness gate, exhausted allocation or completed comparison. Deliver the derivation, fits, all comparisons, independent quadrature, resource receipt and failure/success decision. The original Gaussian can continue as an empirical biological-screening hypothesis, with its physical limitation retained.

## Sparse access and coefficient arithmetic replace one explicit loading table

**Question.** Can a coherent circuit load the contact-derived Hermite factor and residue amplitudes from smaller input arrays without expanding the complete polynomial-by-reduced-rank table? The first construction will use a sparse-access block encoding with reversible occupation arithmetic, followed by the existing factor-based walk, Chebyshev linear combination and overlap readout. This is a specified construction to attempt, not a claim of efficient access.

The existing experiment provides a physical coordinate factor `F`, a covariance eigenbasis `V`, and residue Hermite coefficients through degree four. For occupation vector `α` with total degree one through four, its lifted factor has entries

`L[(r,η),α] = (FV)[r,a] sqrt(α_a)` when `η = α−e_a`.

The first intended access construction is the sparse row/column/value-oracle block encoding of [Gilyén, Su, Low and Wiebe, Lemma 48](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1806.01838), applied after zero padding the rectangular factor. Row positions are enumerated by adding one occupation. Column positions are enumerated by lowering an occupied coordinate and selecting a physical factor row. The value oracle loads a signed `(FV)[r,a]` from the explicitly costed coordinate-factor table and multiplies it by `sqrt(α_a)`, with the finite set of occupation square roots encoded explicitly. Dense covariance fitting, diagonalization, the rigid-coordinate map and the `FV` table remain charged classical inputs. No quantum random-access memory is assumed free.

For `R` factor rows and `d` retained coordinates, safe declared sparsities are `s_row=d` and `s_column=R min(d,4)`. Use a checked entry bound `M ≥ 2 max|FV|` and block normalization `a_L=M sqrt(s_row s_column)`. The factor product then uses normalization `γ=a_L²`, which may be worse than the old Frobenius normalization. Verify the projected factor and product blocks before inserting them into the walk. The old walk angles and Chebyshev degree cannot be reused without recalculation. Avoiding explicit row-norm preparation by this sparse-access construction does not remove its normalization penalty.

**Observable preparation.** Start with a uniform superposition over padded degree-one-to-four occupation addresses. Reversibly decode each occupation and evaluate the published contact polynomial coefficient `h[α,i]`, summing incident contact contributions with the same degree average and harmonic normalization. Store only the contact/coordinate arrays and the finite degree-one-to-four contraction instructions. A controlled signed rotation with amplitude `h[α,i]/H_i`, followed by uncomputation, prepares the desired residue state conditional on its flag. `H_i` is a conservative absolute coefficient bound determined without biological labels. Record the actual success probability `||h_i||²/(D_pad H_i²)` and charge retries or any separately compiled amplification, including inverse preparation. Compute the norm from the independent Gaussian static kernel and charge that computation. A scalar coefficient routine or a successful classical truth-table evaluation alone is insufficient evidence of coherent state preparation.

**Finite prototype.** Begin with the existing ABL residues 242–244 and two retained coordinates, which have fourteen nonconstant Hermite functions. Only after the complete loading and response checks pass, attempt ABL residues 242–245 with the first three and then the first four positive harmonic coordinates, using the unchanged contact rule and separately fitted Gaussian covariance. Each extension is a small newly defined Gaussian fixture, not the 252-residue ABL protein model. If a fixture lacks the required positive modes or connected contacts, retain that qualification failure and do not pick a more favourable fragment. The complete degree-one-to-four bases have 34 functions at `d=3` and 69 at `d=4`, so exhaustive independent classical reference arrays remain finite.

Compile first at 8 and 12 fractional bits, with enough recorded integer/sign bits to prevent overflow. Include a 20-fractional-bit compilation/resource study only: its value register plus addresses, flags and arithmetic workspace already exceeds the 20-qubit statevector cap. Do not promise a 20-bit complete statevector simulation. Even an 8- or 12-bit circuit is simulated only if its actual total registers fit. No selected bit count guarantees response precision. Compare every computed coefficient, every factor column and every prepared observable state that can be simulated with a separately constructed dense reference. Save rounding and truncation discrepancies and distinguish classical arithmetic checks from complete coherent-state checks. Compare all symmetry-distinct residue-pair responses, including diagonal entries, at `0.01τ` and `0.1τ`: twelve queries for the three-residue fixture and twenty for each four-residue extension. Use the fixture's fixed original harmonic `τ` and standard deviations. Require complete deterministic response error at most `0.0005` before projecting a total `0.002` precision plan. The remaining total allowance must explicitly include statistical error and any claimed hardware bias. Ideal simulations do not qualify a hardware bias budget.

**Resource and comparison rule.** Proposed 22 December–18 January activity: at most 48 person-hours, suggested allocation Aniruddh 32 and Çağlar 16, and 64 CPU-worker hours. Remain within 8 GiB process memory, 20 statevector qubits, 200,000 native operations per complete data circuit, 90 seconds per simulation and Chebyshev degree 32. Count all ancillas and arithmetic registers. Compilation or complete-state verification can therefore fail even at the first fixture; do not conceal this by reporting only an arithmetic component as an executed response. No QPU spending or new GPU campaign is part of this loading prototype.

The matched classical methods receive identical coordinates, covariance, observables, times, precision and query workload. Report cold preparation and repeated-query costs separately for cached spectral evaluation, exponential action and analytic Gaussian contractions. Include input construction, coefficient/norm evaluation, block normalization, preparation success probability, forward/inverse arithmetic, approximation degree, readout repetitions, routing when applicable, and classical pre/postprocessing. Report scalar, fixed eight-entry and full-matrix workloads, retaining cases with fewer than eight distinct entries at their actual size. Do not compare simulator wall time with projected QPU wall time.

**Failure deliverable.** Stop at the first resource or correctness gate that prevents a complete calculation. Deliver implemented oracles, component verification, complete-circuit or compiler failure record, memory/gate/normalization/readout ledger and the strongest measured classical reference. Continue toward protein access only if measured component costs plus a fully stated scaling calculation identify a competitive regime. Lack of a competitive regime is a valid completed result of this feasibility test, not evidence of quantum advantage or a universal impossibility result. No full-protein advantage claim follows from passing the small fixtures.

## A bounded inventory precedes an external estimation pilot

**Available source universe.** Use ASD2023 experimental protein–modulator records and their linked primary studies. The [ASD2023 paper](https://academic.oup.com/nar/article/52/D1/D376/7327070) distinguishes experimental entries from its predicted human pocketome. The [official ASD download page](https://mdl.shsmu.edu.cn/ASD/module/download/download.jsp?tabIndex=1) lists protein XML release 5.01, dated 1 June 2023, as `ASD_Release_202306_XF.tar.gz`. This is an identified source inventory, not a locally acquired archive or an admitted independent cohort. Record download access, permitted use, archive checksum and contents at acquisition. The download page restricts research-data redistribution, so no raw ASD archive is promised in the public release. Public evidence should identify sources and report independently curated results within the applicable permissions. Predicted pocketome entries do not become experimental labels. This plan has not inspected new numerical biological outcomes.

**First inventory, 17–30 November.** Within a proposed 48-person-hour allocation, suggested Promit 16, Dennis 8, Çağlar 16 and Aniruddh 8, list all source records with structural accessions and an experimental allosteric-modulator citation. These allocations fit the two-week commitments of 8, 4, 10 and 16 hours per week, respectively. Screen up to forty distinct source families in accession order after the exclusions below, stopping when the allotted hours are exhausted even if fewer than forty have been screened. Preserve the complete machine-readable source index and every manually screened candidate/reason subject to the source permissions. The first twelve eligible families in that order form the maximum pilot panel. Twelve is an engineering ceiling for this first pilot, not a claim that twelve eligible independent families already exist or that this number is powered. If fewer qualify, retain the smaller count. If no eligible families remain, deliver the inventory and availability limitation without substituting development examples.

Each inventory row records source release/accession, primary paper and functional assay, structure and assembly, sequence/domain identifiers, model-input/reference states, receiver source, coverage, contact-label source and exposure history. Dennis will curate label evidence and Promit will review input/assay compatibility as proposed responsibilities within the confirmed team effort. If either or another prediction-team member has already inspected a family's labels, that family cannot be described as an untouched test family. Public-source label separation is operational masking for a retrospective analysis, not a prospective experimental outcome.

**Independence and exclusions.** Before predictions, reconcile the new source index against the complete Pulsar development and prior validation inventory, including KRAS/RAS, ABL/kinase, MYC/MAX, MYH7/myosin, GRB2/SH3 and other previously inspected families. These examples are a minimum exclusion list, not a claim that they exhaust past exposure. Add the original [Ohm twenty-protein evaluation set](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-020-17618-2) and the [AlloPred training/testing inventory](https://github.com/jgreener64/allopred/tree/master/dataset) to the overlap check. AlloPred remains secondary unless its pocket-to-residue adapter is separately specified. Group homologous domains conservatively using the same recorded family classification and a sequence/structure review; different PDB identifiers alone never establish independence. Assign one primary structure/reference state per family before scoring. Additional states are sensitivities within that family.

**Input admission.** Require a separately justified functional receiver, compatible construct and functional state, at least five candidates after receiver exclusion and the existing 6 Å receiver-distance rule, mapped reference residues and an attainable numerical representation. Genuine experimentally unliganded input is preferred. A ligand-stripped bound structure is explicitly identified as such and cannot support an apo-only generalization. Resolve missing atoms, assembly mismatch and receiver ambiguity before admission. Record failed candidates rather than selecting on predictions. If several structures qualify, choose by receiver completeness, required-atom coverage, experimental-method-specific quality, then accession order, before viewing scores.

**Primary endpoint.** Estimate the equal-family mean difference in precision@5 between Gaussian response and the fixed Ohm adapter, for contacts to a primary-study-supported allosteric ligand. A primary positive is an eligible residue with at least one mapped heavy atom within 4.5 Å of that specified ligand in the eligible reference. This threshold is a proposed localization definition, not a universal functional threshold. Retain all five nominations; do not substitute sixth-ranked residues. The Ohm source, seeds 11/29/47, arithmetic-mean aggregation, receiver map and candidate mask follow `docs/delivery/biological-validation-design.md`. Harmonic response, degree and receiver distance are prespecified supporting comparisons. Contact localization does not establish that an individual nominated mutation regulates function.

**Controls and unknowns.** A known noncontact requires sufficient mapped heavy-atom coverage to exclude the same 4.5 Å reference contact. Unresolved mapping or atoms create an unknown label, not a negative. Assess localization specificity with noncontacts matched to receiver distance, contact degree and solvent exposure using an input-only rule selected before score access. An unannotated pocket is not an inactive biological control. A genuinely inactive perturbation requires a primary functional assay with a negligible-effect margin and sufficiently narrow measurement uncertainty. If such controls cannot be found, report the absence and retain the contact-specific interpretation. Any subgroup or alternative contact cutoff is exploratory unless specified before labels.

**Uncertainty and pilot status.** Report family differences and precision@5 label-identification intervals. For unknown residues use one shared label assignment for both methods and the exact lower/upper difference bounds already defined in the biological design. Retain failed admitted families in coverage and bound missing outputs rather than assigning a failed comparator zero precision. For fully observed families, provide the equal-family mean and paired 95% t interval, explicitly conditional on independent, approximately normal family differences. Also show the conservative bounded-difference interval and the exact paired sign count. Unknown-label outcomes use identification bounds plus the conservative interval and are not replaced by midpoint t intervals. These uncertainty analyses need implementation checks before label joins.

The existing analytic planning study gives a nominal 95% half-width of 0.10 at 27 families when the paired standard deviation is assumed to be 0.25. The assumption is not an observed external variance. Therefore a pilot capped at twelve families cannot promise that precision. The pilot remains descriptive estimation whenever independence is incomplete, the count or uncertainty is inadequate, unknown labels persist, or an apparent gain does not survive the declared analysis. A ten-percentage-point gain remains a proposed practical target, not a programme rule or an achieved effect. This sprint will not expand or replace the panel after seeing a favourable or unfavourable result. No wet-lab capacity is assumed.
