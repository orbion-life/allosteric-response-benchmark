## Three levels of reproduction

1. Repository-only checks validate the GRB2 saved evidence and mathematical implementations, temporal protocols and fresh tiny pulse, and quantum lookup/phase semantics. Three new Linux workflows describe their exact scope.
2. Asset-based offline checks validate original arrays and compiled circuits. The temporal reconstruction creates a fresh tree and reconstructs 28 derived waveform archives from eight raw kernels and fixed operators. The corrected peak-pair supplement supersedes the peak diagnostics in those historical waveforms; it leaves response matrices, ranking and pass/fail outcomes unchanged.
3. Scientific regeneration recomputes Gaussian kernels or circuit synthesis using each asset's documented environment. Full biological source replay additionally requires acquiring the pinned publisher workbook under its terms. No QPU execution is included in the new experiments.

A passing source or reconstruction check is not a passing scientific threshold. All twelve broad pulse tests fail. The 69.2% T-count reduction applies to a nine-dimensional model and strongest evaluated dense implementation; it increases CX count and workspace. The phase-gradient improvement has not been compiled on the full protein operators. The primary KRAS lookup plan's roughly 1.55e20 T upper estimate remains in the evidence.
