# Literature supporting the response model and quantum estimation study

Version 0.9.1 is a report and documentation patch. It connects two biological precedents and a conditional quantum algorithm to the proposed calculations. Scientific code, raw data and numerical results remain unchanged from [v0.9.0](https://github.com/orbion-life/allosteric-response-benchmark/releases/tag/v0.9.0).

| Primary source | Connection to the proposed work | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| [Rodgers et al., 2013](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.1001651) | Experimentally tested elastic-network predictions motivate fluctuation-based screening. | This precedent does not qualify our Gaussian approximation or resolve its nonlinear-reference discrepancy. |
| [Greener and Sternberg, 2015](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12859-015-0771-1) | AlloPred combines mechanical perturbations with pocket descriptors. | Its pocket-level result is not a forecast of our residue-level precision or evidence of independent Pulsar benefit. |
| [Kharazi et al., 2026, preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15523v2) | Direct dissipative overlaps motivate estimating the time-dependent term in the reduced response. | State preparation, operator access, normalization and readout must be implemented and costed. The full response also includes a static subtraction. |

The quantum comparison retains [classical matrix-exponential action](https://doi.org/10.1137/100788860) using the same factors and accuracy. The complete cost comparison, rather than an isolated query bound, determines whether the proposed quantum branch should continue.

Report changes are confined to the rationale on pages 2 and 4 and the reference/provenance text on page 9. The other six pages are unchanged in text and rendered pixels. All eight numbered equations, figures, numerical results and team information are preserved. The report still contains six main pages and three appendices.
